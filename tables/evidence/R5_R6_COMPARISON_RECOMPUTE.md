## Recalculation

For each task and case, we computed the arithmetic mean of `difference_REAL3_minus_POLICY` over the 12 R5 rows (4 arms × 3 streams). The reported estimate is the mean of these 100 case means. The interval is the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the 5,000 case-bootstrap means generated by the frozen implementation.

## Interpretation

The R5 cross-check and R6 fresh-case result overlap in their conditional intervals. This is a descriptive cross-delivery comparison, not a new experiment or a claim of equivalence. The R5 cross-check is a post hoc computation on sealed rows; R6 retains its preregistered evidence level.
